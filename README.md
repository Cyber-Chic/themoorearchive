### THE EXPERIMENT & LATEST PHASE
This project began as a quiet experiment to explore how small, anonymous acts of kindness resonate in the world. While the formal study has formally [ended](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/400971211_The_Moore_Archive_An_Inquiry_into_Invisible_Action_and_the_Resonance_of_Kindness), **the quest to spread joy around the world prevails!** 

***

### GEOCACHE STATUS: DRIFT MODE
* **Open to the Community:** The geocache portion of the project is now in permanent **Drift Mode** and belongs entirely to the community. 
* **The Digital Key & Physical Leaks:** The physical key was designed to unlock a hidden digital vault of art and verse. While this digital vault remains locked to those who cannot locate the key, the archive leaks beautifully into the physical world through **Free Little Art Galleries (FLAGs)** and libraries. Explore this repository to learn more about the creative origins of the project.
