Studio Fellowship

About the Fellowship

The Studio Fellowship at the Moore Signal Archive is a structured space for independent research and creative projects. The framework focuses on the intersection of art and science.

Through this fellowship, we look at open research data from space, using it to inspire cosmic poetry and artistic pieces that are made accessible to global communities.

What We Do

  • Data & Research: Exploring public astro-citizen science data to find creative ways to share it with the public.
  • Public Art: Sending free books and original artwork to different cities around the globe in order to build community.
  • Outer Space: Curating a unique and immersive cosmic exhibition comprised of original literature and stellar visuals.

Background

The core projects behind this fellowship were first presented to the public at the Ely Center of Contemporary Art as an official showcase during CT Tech Week.
