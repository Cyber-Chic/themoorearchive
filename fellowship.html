About the Fellowship

The Studio Fellowship at the Moore Signal Archive is a structured space for independent research and creative projects focusing on the intersection of art, science, and space data.

What We Do

  • Data & Research: Exploring public astro-citizen science data.
  • Public Art: Sharing free books and original artwork globally.
  • Outer Space: Curating cosmic exhibitions and literature.

"Quietly lend a hand, and change the world your own powerful way!"
